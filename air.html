A Landscape
of Repair

Year2023LocationGenoa, ItalySiteHennebique SilosTypeAdaptive Reuse / Public改修 / 公共CollaborationLeon Kallert + Yusuke Matsuzaki
Spatial study / new supporting structure01 / Selected Work
Project

A building on the verge of collapse. More than 200 metres long and 40 metres wide, the Hennebique Silos have been exposed for decades to the corrosive salt air of Genoa's harbour. The project asks how a neglected structure can be kept from falling apart while being returned to public life.

崩壊の瀬戸際にある巨大建築。全長200mを超え、幅約40mのHennebique Silosは、ジェノヴァ港の塩分を含む空気に数十年さらされてきた。本計画は、放置された構造体を単に保存するのではなく、崩壊を防ぎながら再び公共の生活へ開く方法を考える。

A Landscape of Repair treats repair not as an invisible technical operation, but as an architectural act. Existing holes and new points of failure become opportunities for access. New supporting structures stabilise the building while becoming part of its spatial language.

A Landscape of Repairでは、補修を見えない技術行為ではなく、建築そのものをつくる行為として扱う。既存の欠損や新たな破損をアクセスの契機として読み替え、付加する支持構造を建築の空間言語そのものにする。

01 / Existing Condition

Repair begins
with what exists

Decay is read as information: where to support, where to open, and where new public access can attach.

劣化を消去するのではなく、どこを支え、どこを開き、どこに公共動線を接続するかを示す情報として読む。

Repair is treated not as a return to an original state, but as a way to produce a new public condition.
補修を元の状態へ戻す行為ではなく、新しい公共性を生み出す方法として扱う。
02 / Strategy

Structure as
public armature

A secondary frame grows around and through the existing concrete mass. Bracing, stairs and platforms act as stabilisation, circulation and places to occupy.

既存のコンクリート躯体を縫うように二次フレームを付加し、ブレース、階段、足場に補強・動線・滞在という複数の役割を与える。

03 / Time + Programme

An architecture
that remains open

The silo is not frozen into a single finished programme. A long-term framework allows activities to occupy the structure with different rhythms over time.

サイロを単一用途へ固定せず、活動が異なる時間軸で入り替わる長期的なプログラムの枠組みを与える。
